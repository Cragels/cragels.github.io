About me

Hello, my name is Craig Harrod and I am a junior game developer and designer based in Cape Town. I am about to graduate from Vega School (Emeris).

Skills and Tools

C# C++ Kotlin Blender Unity Unreal Engine 5 Game Design 3D Modelling Digital Sculpting 3D Animation Rigging Texturing Topology Environment Concept Design UI Design

Education

IIE Bachelor of Computer and Information Sciences
in Game Design and Development

Throughout my studies at Vega I have learnt a wide range of skills for video game development and design. I am very comfortable moving from designing and creating in software like Blender then exporting all types of art assets over to a game engine like Unity or Unreal and carry on working in them.

I enjoy creating systems that are modular which allows for easy collaboration. I take great care in making assets and environments with clean structured topology.

I find all aspects of digital art enjoyable which leads me to constantly be improving and learning new skills. Having worked on many game jams and assignments there is no better feeling than having a completed game that is playable and enjoyed by others.
